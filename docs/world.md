# Grand Theft Auto VII: World & Regions

## Overview
The Free State of New Vegas is a sprawling, multi-biome open world that blends the densest urban playground Rockstar has ever attempted with extreme climate and technological contrasts. The map is designed so that every region supports different playstyles, reputation strategies, and mission types.

---

## Core Regions

### 1. New Vegas Proper & The Strip
**Tone:** Hyper-modern, neon-saturated, always-on surveillance capitalism.

- Vertical mega-casinos that double as data centers and server farms
- AI-run resorts with autonomous staff and facial-recognition everything
- "Smart" streets that dynamically change traffic patterns, lighting, and advertising based on player actions and reputation
- High Online Fame and Corporate Heat zones
- Best for: Social engineering, VIP infiltration, livestream distractions, high-stakes digital heists

**Key Locations**
- Luxor-9 Data Resort (major mid-game heist target)
- The Neon Spire (tallest structure, corporate HQ and observation deck)
- Algorithm Alley (underground black-market app developers and deepfake studios)

### 2. The Strip’s Shadow (Undercity)
**Tone:** Dark, humid, rebellious, offline by design.

- Massive subterranean network of service tunnels, abandoned maintenance levels, and illegal expansions
- Server farms, black-market neural-link clinics, drone-swarm fight clubs
- Primary hub for Offline Respect progression
- Fast-travel network once unlocked via Offline Respect
- Best for: EMP warfare, resistance contact missions, escaping the surface surveillance grid

**Key Locations**
- The Dead Zone (complete signal blackout area)
- Neural Chop Shop (illegal body-mod and neural-link clinic)
- Swarm Pits (illegal drone combat arenas)

### 3. The Dustbowl Expanses
**Tone:** Harsh, analog, sun-baked survival.

- Solar farm scrapyards, abandoned industrial sites, temporary "glamp" cities for the ultra-rich fleeing coastal flooding
- Stronghold of Street Cred and Offline Respect
- Classic line-of-sight police response (human deputies and old cruisers)
- Best for: Physical heists, vehicle combat, analog weapon crafting, gang alliances

**Key Locations**
- Scraptown (major black-market trading post)
- The Glamp Ring (exclusive temporary luxury camps that can be robbed or protected)
- Old Missile Silo Complex (potential late-game base or multi-stage heist location)

### 4. High-Desert National Parks
**Tone:** Wild, open, high-speed.

- Illegal off-road racing circuits, abandoned military testing grounds, hidden smuggling routes
- Excellent for vehicle customization and high-speed pursuits
- Sparse surveillance — good place to cool off Corporate Heat
- Best for: Racing, long-distance getaways, outdoor ambushes

### 5. Militarized Southern Border
**Tone:** Tense, heavily fortified, high-stakes smuggling.

- Checkpoints, border wall canyons, drone patrols, and human/AI hybrid security forces
- Major smuggling corridor for both physical goods and data
- High Corporate Heat and Street Cred intersection
- Best for: High-risk smuggling missions, border crossings, large-scale escapes

### 6. Los Santos Metro Satellite Zone
**Tone:** Dense, coastal, more traditional GTA urban feel with corporate outposts.

- Expanded and modernized version of classic Los Santos districts
- Mix of human and drone law enforcement
- Corporate research campuses and older residential neighborhoods
- Serves as a bridge between the old San Andreas world and the new New Vegas reality
- Best for: Story missions that connect the past to the present, mid-tier heists, character backstory exploration

## Regional Mechanics Summary

| Region                  | Dominant Reputation     | Law Response Type          | Best Playstyle              |
|-------------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------|-----------------------------|
| New Vegas Proper        | Online Fame / Corporate  | Fully autonomous AI        | Social engineering, hacking |
| The Strip’s Shadow      | Offline Respect          | Minimal / EMP vulnerable   | Resistance, stealth         |
| Dustbowl Expanses       | Street Cred              | Human deputies             | Physical combat, gangs      |
| High-Desert Parks       | Mixed / Low surveillance | Sparse drones              | Racing, getaways            |
| Southern Border         | Street + Corporate       | Hybrid human/AI            | Smuggling, high risk        |
| Los Santos Satellite    | Balanced                 | Mixed human + drones       | Story, mid-tier crime       |

## Design Intent
Every region is built to reward different protagonists and different reputation paths. The map encourages players to move between zones deliberately rather than treating the whole world as a uniform sandbox. High Online Fame makes the Strip extremely powerful but the Dustbowl hostile. High Street Cred opens the underworld but locks you out of VIP areas. Offline Respect is the only reliable way to travel freely beneath the surveillance grid.

This creates constant strategic tension and supports the core fantasy of three very different criminals forced to work together.
