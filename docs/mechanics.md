# Grand Theft Auto VII: Mechanics & Systems

## Overview
Moving beyond the traditional linear "Wanted Level," *GTA VII* introduces the **Quad-Reputation System**. In the hyper-connected Free State of New Vegas, every action is recorded, analyzed, and monetized. The system forces players to balance conflicting metrics, as succeeding in one world often means burning bridges in another.

---

## 1. The Quad-Reputation System

### 📱 Online Fame (The Influencer Economy)
* **How to Gain:** Executing viral public stunts, hijacking digital billboards, completing corporate sponsorships, and manipulating social media feeds (primarily via Kai).
* **Benefits:** Unlocks high-end drone deliveries, VIP access to exclusive Mega-Casinos, luxury clothing brands, and the ability to summon flash mobs for distractions.
* **The Catch:** You become instantly recognizable. Standard stealth missions become nearly impossible as pedestrians will constantly stop to film you, unintentionally alerting security.

### 🔪 Street Cred (The Analog Underworld)
* **How to Gain:** Traditional heists, underground fight clubs, fencing stolen analog goods, and physical intimidation (primarily via Brick).
* **Benefits:** Unlocks black-market weapon dealers, street-level getaway drivers, safehouses in the Dustbowl Expanses, and loyalty from old-school gangs.
* **The Catch:** High Street Cred triggers automatic profiling from smart-city surveillance. Corporate sponsors will drop you, and high-end districts will flag you as a threat upon entry.

### 🌐 Corporate Heat (The Automated Threat)
* **How to Gain:** Hacking infrastructure, destroying automated delivery vehicles, and stealing data from AI-run resorts (primarily via Amara).
* **The Penalty:** Replaces the standard police response. Instead of human cops, the city dispatches autonomous pursuit vehicles, facial-recognition drones, and heavily armored corporate strike teams. 
* **Mitigation:** Requires deepfake spoofing or EMP blasts to clear. Hiding in an alley won't work if your biometric signature is still broadcasting.

### 🕶️ Offline Respect (The Resistance)
* **How to Gain:** Destroying cellular towers, sabotaging data centers, and aiding the anti-AI underground movement.
* **Benefits:** Grants access to the "Undercity" tunnel networks, providing fast travel beneath the surveillance grid. Unlocks EMP weaponry and signal jammers.

---

## 2. Character Synergies & Conflicts

Because no single character can maximize every reputation meter without crippling their gameplay style, players must use the protagonists strategically:

* **The Bait and Switch:** Use Kai (High Online Fame) to walk through the front door of a corporate gala and distract the room with a livestream. Meanwhile, use Brick (Zero Digital Footprint) to infiltrate the basement vault completely undetected by the algorithms.
* **Reputation Laundering:** If Brick accumulates too much Street Cred and can't enter the city center, players can use Amara to hack the AuraMind database and temporarily erase his criminal profile, effectively "laundering" his reputation for a single mission.

---

## 3. The "Smart" Wanted Level
When committing crimes, the response scales based on the district's technological level:
1. **The Dustbowl:** Old-school sheriff cruisers and human deputies. Classic line-of-sight escapes work here.
2. **Los Santos Satellite Zone:** A mix of human LAPD equivalents and basic drone support. 
3. **New Vegas Proper:** Fully autonomous AI police force. To escape, players must either out-hack the pursuers, utilize an EMP, or flee into a subterranean dead zone where the signal drops.
