# Grand Theft Auto VII: Characters & Skill Trees

## Overview
The protagonist framework for *Grand Theft Auto VII* relies on three distinct perspectives, each tied to a specific tier of the 2030s social and criminal hierarchy. Their individual skill trees directly influence how missions are approached, what resources are available, and how the world reacts.

---

### 1. Kai “Ghost” Rivera (The Social Engineer)
* **Archetype:** The fallen influencer navigating public perception and corporate digital networks.
* **Core Focus:** Crowd manipulation, social engineering, and diversion tactics.

#### Skill Tree
* **Viral Amplification**
  * *Level 1:* Increases the speed at which civilian crowds gather around public stunts.
  * *Level 2:* Allows remote hijacking of digital billboards to project localized visual cover or distractions.
  * *Level 3:* **"Flash Mob" Ability** — Forces nearby NPCs to record and swarm an area, completely blocking corporate security vehicles.
* **Deepfake Deception**
  * *Level 1:* Spoofs low-level facial recognition cameras for brief windows.
  * *Level 2:* Simulates audio/video commands to trick automated customer service kiosks and smart-doors.
  * *Level 3:* **"Ghost Persona"** — Temporarily tricks high-tier corporate security networks into treating Kai as a certified VIP.
* **Brand Sponsorships**
  * *Level 1:* Generates passive in-game cryptocurrency/cash based on current online fame metrics.
  * *Level 2:* Unlocks exclusive high-end, hyper-customized luxury vehicles delivered via drone.

---

### 2. Dr. Amara Solis (The Cybernetic Ghost)
* **Archetype:** The tech-savvy whistleblower utilizing deep-system knowledge to bypass physical security.
* **Core Focus:** Hardware hacking, drone control, and electronic warfare.

#### Skill Tree
* **Grid Overdrive**
  * *Level 1:* Extends the duration of the slow-motion hacking interface.
  * *Level 2:* Enables remote locking/unlocking of autonomous civilian and corporate vehicles.
  * *Level 3:* **"System Override"** — Forces enemy automated drones and turret systems to switch sides and target hostile forces.
* **Signal Jamming**
  * *Level 1:* Disrupts local police radio chatter, delaying reinforcement arrival times.
  * *Level 2:* Blinds regional surveillance cameras in a designated radius.
  * *Level 3:* **"Total Blackout"** — Triggers a localized power grid failure in a targeted city block, plunging the area into darkness.
* **Data Splicing**
  * *Level 1:* Uncovers encrypted side paths, hidden tunnels, and hidden server caches on the mini-map.
  * *Level 2:* Bypasses high-security biometric locks without physical keys or cards.

---

### 3. Marcus “Brick” Hale (The Old-School Anchor)
* **Archetype:** The grounded, analog-reliant veteran who rejects modern digital conveniences.
* **Core Focus:** Heavy combat, physical infiltration, and high-durability survival.

#### Skill Tree
* **Adrenaline Surge**
  * *Level 1:* Increases close-quarters combat damage and execution speed.
  * *Level 2:* Reduces incoming physical and ballistic damage during active firefights.
  * *Level 3:* **"Juggernaut Stance"** — Temporarily grants near-total resistance to standard gunfire and enables devastating heavy-weapon handling.
* **Analog Resistance**
  * *Level 1:* Generates zero automated digital heat; police and corporate systems take significantly longer to flag Brick.
  * *Level 2:* Immune to electronic EMP traps and remote tracking devices.
* **Heavy Arsenal Mastery**
  * *Level 1:* Increases reload speed and ammunition capacity for shotguns, LMGs, and explosives.
  * *Level 2:* **"Improvised Demolitions"** — Unlocks the ability to craft high-yield analog explosives from scrap and industrial components.
