## Detailed Protagonist Profiles

### 1. Kai “Ghost” Rivera (The Social Engineer)

* **Background**: Former darling of the New Vegas influencer scene, Kai built a multi-million-dollar empire on lifestyle vlogs, synthetic persona curation, and high-fashion brand deals. His life imploded overnight when a rival collective exposed his deepfake use and financial fraud.
* **Special Ability**: Viral Manipulation. Can hijack local public displays, stream feeds, or NPC attention. Activating his ability causes nearby civilians to whip out their phones and swarm an area, creating human roadblocks, flash mobs, or distracting corporate security.
* **Starting Stats**: High Online Reputation, High Corporate Awareness, Zero Street Cred.

### 2. Dr. Amara Solis (The Cybernetic Ghost)

* **Background**: Lead systems architect for AuraMind Corp, Amara designed the foundational neural architecture for the city's automated infrastructure. When she discovered the system was quietly weaponizing citizen behavioral data to eliminate dissent, she leaked the files and went deep underground.
* **Special Ability**: Overdrive Hack. Can pause time momentarily to interface with any electronic device, vehicle, or security grid within line of sight. She can force enemy drones to switch sides, jam police radios, or cause autonomous luxury cars to lock their doors and drive into walls.
* **Starting Stats**: High Tech Mastery, Low Online Reputation, Moderate Underground Respect.

### 3. Marcus “Brick” Hale (The Old-School Anchor)

* **Background**: A career stick-up man who cut his teeth in San Andreas decades ago. After serving a lengthy sentence and watching the criminal underworld get taken over by script-kiddies, algorithms, and crypto-scammers, Brick stays entirely off-grid. He doesn't own a smartphone, refuses neural links, and relies on cash, muscle, and analog gear.
* **Special Ability**: Adrenaline Surge. Enters a state of heightened physical combat readiness, taking drastically reduced damage and unleashing devastating close-quarters combat takedowns. His absolute lack of digital footprint also means he takes much longer to accumulate automated police heat.
* **Starting Stats**: Maximum Street Cred, High Physical Toughness, Zero Digital Footprint.

## Sample Mission Outline: "Algorithmic Asset Stripping"

* **Act**: Mid-Game
* **Objective**: Infiltrate a subterranean server-casino beneath the Luxor-9 Data Resort to steal a physical quantum drive containing the AI's core training weights before a rival corporation deletes it.

### Phase 1: Preparation & Approach

* **Kai's Angle**: Poses as a tech-titan VIP attending an exclusive rooftop party to bypass facial recognition gates and disable internal lockdown protocols using social clout.
* **Amara's Angle**: Sits in a parked van outside, intercepting local drone feeds and spoofing the casino's automated grid to grant the team fake security clearance.
* **Brick's Angle**: Descends through the ventilation maintenance shafts with heavy-duty thermal charges to secure the physical extraction point in the basement server vaults.

### Phase 2: The Complication

As Brick lifts the quantum drive, the autonomous crime AI recognizes the breach and triggers the casino's Dynamic Threat Response. The smart-streets above lock down, local automated police cruisers deploy without human drivers, and Kai's face is blasted across every digital billboard in New Vegas with a massive bounty attached.

### Phase 3: The Escape

* Players seamlessly switch between characters during a high-speed vehicle chase across the neon-lit Strip.
* Amara hacks enemy pursuit drones, sending them crashing into streetlamps.
* Kai broadcasts a live-stream manipulation stunt, inciting thousands of local fans to flood the boulevard in customized lowriders, creating gridlock that blocks corporate strike teams.
* Brick mans the heavy turret in the truck bed, clearing a path through automated roadblocks into the Dustbowl Expanses.

## Radio Station Concepts

### 98.1 W-WURD (The Offline Frequency)
Pirate radio broadcasting out of the Dustbowl. Plays raw, gritty underground punk, old-school West Coast hip-hop, and anti-corporate spoken word. DJs constantly rant about solar flares, paper currency, and "the meat-space revolution."

### Neon Pulse FM (Las Venturas Hits)
Ultra-polished synthwave, hyperpop, and EDM curated by hyper-synthetic AI algorithms that change the beat and lyrics based on real-time weather and player crime levels in the world.

### Silicon Talk & Spun Out
A talk-radio station parodying tech-bro culture, venture capitalism, and doomsday preppers. Features unhinged callers debating whether downloading consciousness into a slot machine constitutes an afterlife.
